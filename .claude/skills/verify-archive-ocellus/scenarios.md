# Scenarios and their oracles

This page lists, for each mechanic, the Vitest scenario and the browser scenario that prove it, the input each one sends, and where its expected value comes from. Use it before moving code between owners. A mechanic whose rows lose a scenario, or whose oracle becomes a value read from the code under test, has lost its safety net. Change this page in the same pull request that changes a scenario.

## Input kinds

- `click`: a real `mousedown` on the trainer's canvas at the SDK's 2D projection of the tile, `Viewport.projectTile`. `drill.click(tile, { ctrlKey })` in `src/content/ocellus/tests/drill.ts` sends it, and page tests send the same click with `clickTile`. The SDK's ClickController resolves the tile, queues the order through InputController, and the next `world.tickWorld()` hands it to `Player.moveTo`. The fight log's `walk-ordered` input proves which tile the click resolved to. A click on `UNDER_BOSS` while the boss is drawn attacks him instead, and his `player-hit-judged` events show each attack's launch, landing and requested damage. A click on a void rift's tile attacks the rift. The drill's 2D canvas is 800 by 800 pixels, 12 tiles each way around the player, so a click reaches any tile up to 5 rows from the player.
- `press`: the same real `mousedown` as `click`, sent by `drill.clickRightBeforeTick(tile)`, which calls `world.tickWorld()` right after it with no other task between, as a click in the frame before a tick is in the browser.
- `page`: a real DOM click or key press on the mounted page through `loadPage` in `page.tsx`.
- `order`: a direct `Player.moveTo` through `drill.walkTo` or `drill.player().moveTo`. It skips ClickController, the input queue and the Control-key mapping.
- `none`: no player input. The URL's drill options and real World ticks drive the run.
- Browser: `scripts/verify.sh browser` sends real mouse input through agent-browser, and a Control-click through CDP's Input domain. Every browser scenario runs in 3D except `drive_explosion_views`, which saves `use3dView: false` first and runs its dodge page and a run-end page in 2D, then the run-end page again in 3D. The walkway, back wall, corner, pathing and holes pages click each named tile at the SDK's 3D projection, read through the probe's `tileCentre`, and `projectedClicksOrderTheirTiles` checks that every such click ordered its tile. The other floor and target clicks still use hand-placed offsets from the canvas centre.

Every Vitest scenario built on `startDrill` ticks the real world through GET READY, so the first world tick after `startDrill` or `drill.restart` is P4 tick 0.

## Oracle sources

- `data`: a value in `src/content/ocellus/js/OcellusTimings.ts`, named by its `OCELLUS_P4` key. Each value there carries its own footage, wiki, cache or owner source.
- `wiki`: an Old School RuneScape Wiki page. The data file cites the pages its values come from.
- `hand`: worked out by hand from `data` or `wiki` values, with the working given here.
- `seeded`: the output of a seeded roll, pinned from a passing run. Only its bounds are independent, and they come from `data`.
- `rolls`: a player's attack roll worked out from the seed alone. The combat stream is mulberry32 seeded with the seed XOR `0x5eed0c0b`, drawn once per SDK `Random.get()` in the player's attack step. Each hit draws accuracy first and, when the draw is at most the hit chance, draws damage as ⌊draw × (max + 1)⌋. A Twisted Bow shot on Ocellus has hit chance 44 490 / 162 653 and max hit 62, from the `wiki` ranged formulas with Ranged 99, the ranged setup's +233 attack and +107 strength, and the bow's passive at Ocellus's Magic capped to 250. A scythe swing on Jab hits three times with hit chance 19 960 / 48 693 and maxes 35, 17 and 8, from Attack and Strength 99, the melee setup's +118 crush and +139 strength and Inquisitor's 2.5%. On seed 100 the first shot requests 54 and the first swing 31, 7 and 5. On seed 1 the first six shots request 0, 0, 0, 0, 12 and 33, over 9 draws with the seventh. The same working gives the pinned seed 4 values 8, 44 and 62, and 4, 12 and 8. An arrow from a tile 3 tiles from the boss lands 3 ticks after it is fired, and from 1 or 2 tiles, 2 ticks: the SDK's hit delay ⌊(3 + distance) / 6⌋ + 1 plus the tick the projectile is queued.

## Tests that bypass real input or real ticks

Each stays until the slice that replaces its API, which then moves the claim onto real input and deletes the bypass. An entry that names no slice belongs to a mechanic the approved migration does not move, and it keeps its bypass until a slice is scoped for it. A scan of the TypeScript syntax tree of every `*.test.ts` and `*.test.tsx` file in `src/content/ocellus/tests/` produced the counts and lines below.

- **Direct restarts.** `drill.restart` skips the Restart button, because it calls `region.reset` directly, in `OcellusRegion.test.ts:27` and `:99` and `U1-cycle.test.ts:137`. No slice moves restarts.
- **Direct `Player.moveTo` orders.** `drill.walkTo` and `drill.player().moveTo` in `BossFootprint.test.ts` (10), `OcellusRegion.test.ts` (8), `U8-floor-holes.test.ts` (8), `U2-floor.test.ts` (7), `Invincible.test.tsx` (3), `U1-cycle.test.ts` (2), and one each in `U1_5-loadouts.test.ts`, `U4-explosions.test.ts`, `U5-transition.test.ts`, `U6-overheads-heals-rifts.test.ts` and `U8-boss-defence.test.ts`. `drill.playCorrectlyTo` also orders with `moveTo` when it walks to the ring, back to spawn and off each explosion mark, in `BossFootprint.test.ts`, `BossStates.test.ts`, `U1-cycle.test.ts`, `U3-lifeforce-orb.test.ts` and `U5-transition.test.ts`. A floor tile in view converts to `drill.click`. A tile under the visible boss stays with the browser. The slice that moves each mechanic converts its file. Slice 5 converted the orbs: every order in `U3-lifeforce-orb.test.ts` and `drill.playCorrectlyTo`'s walk to each new orb are clicks. The outer orb table's two-step row, onto (5, 4) under the visible boss, was removed, because the two-step entry guard clicks the same route while the boss is hidden. Slice 6's explosions unit converted `U4-explosions.test.ts` and `U4-dodge-attack-speed.test.ts`. The one order left in `U4-explosions.test.ts` steps onto (5, 5) under the visible boss on a mark tick, where a 2D click attacks him; `drive_explosions` walks under him by click while he is hidden. `drill.playCorrectlyTo` still steps off each mark with `moveTo`. Later slice 6 units convert the floor and rifts.
- **Direct prayer toggles.** `drill.pray` calls `toggle` on the prayer, skipping the prayer tab click, in `U1_5-loadouts.test.ts`, `U1-cycle.test.ts`, `U3-lifeforce-orb.test.ts`, `U5-transition.test.ts` and `U6-overheads-heals-rifts.test.ts`. The P3 drill harness `p3drill.ts` toggles the same way in `P3-drill.test.ts` and `P3-smite.test.ts`; `drive_p3_drill` and `drive_p3_smite` click the prayer tab. `drill.playCorrectlyTo` toggles the same way on each transition tell, in the files listed above.
- **Direct inventory clicks.** `drill.clickInventory` and `item.inventoryLeftClick` skip the side-panel click. Used in `U1_5-loadouts.test.ts`, `U4-dodge-attack-speed.test.ts`, `U6-overheads-heals-rifts.test.ts`, `U7-supplies-and-names.test.ts` and `U8-boss-defence.test.ts`.
- **Direct attacks.** `player().setAggro(target)` picks the target with no click on it, 9 times: `U6-overheads-heals-rifts.test.ts` (3), `U1_5-loadouts.test.ts` (3), `U8-boss-defence.test.ts` (2) and `Invincible.test.tsx:146`. In 2D, a `drill.click` on a tile of the visible boss attacks him, as the phase activation case above records. Slice 4 converted the seeded combat test in `U1-cycle.test.ts` to that click. Slice 6's explosions unit converted the attacks around each dodge in `U4-dodge-attack-speed.test.ts` to it. A later slice 6 unit converts all of `U6-overheads-heals-rifts.test.ts` with the rifts. No slice moves loadouts, boss defence or Invincible.
- **Direct special attacks.** An assignment to `player().useSpecialAttack` skips the click on the special attack bar, 8 times: `U1_5-loadouts.test.ts` (3), `U8-boss-defence.test.ts` (3) and `U6-overheads-heals-rifts.test.ts` (2). Slice 6 converts the `U6` cases with the rifts. No slice moves loadouts or boss defence.
- **Direct attack styles.** `AttackStylesController.controller.setWeaponAttackStyle` skips the click on the combat tab, in `U1_5-loadouts.test.ts:115`, `:128` and `:133`. No slice moves loadouts.
- **Direct key binding start.** `SettingsControls.instance.startKeyBinding("inventory")` skips the click on the inventory key binding button in the settings panel, in `U7-keybinds.test.tsx:26` and `:47`. No slice moves key bindings.
- **Synthetic hits.** `drill.hit` adds a projectile with a chosen damage, twice, both on a rift in `U6-overheads-heals-rifts.test.ts`: the kill timing test at `:282` and the `rift-hit` guard at `:300`. Slice 6 converts them with the rifts and deletes `drill.hit`. Slice 4 converted the six in `U1-cycle.test.ts`, the Deflect launch-tick table and the Soul Split kill in `U6-overheads-heals-rifts.test.ts`, and `hitBoss` in `RunResult.test.tsx` to clicks on the boss that land real attacks from the seeded combat stream, and deleted `hitBoss`.
- **Direct state writes.** `region.invincible` in `Invincible.test.tsx` (7) and `U1-cycle.test.ts:8`. `currentStats.strength` in `U1_5-loadouts.test.ts:107` and `:116`. `currentStats.prayer` in `U7-supplies-and-names.test.ts:48`. `Settings.playsAudio` and `Settings.playsAreaAudio` in `Cues.test.ts:61` and `:62`, and `Settings.inventory_key` in `U7-keybinds.test.tsx:32`, leave stale values in memory before a reload. A browser reload rebuilds `Settings` from storage instead. No slice moves any of these writes.

Some test calls only observe. The spies on `playAnimation` in `U1-cycle.test.ts`, on `SoundCache.play` in `Cues.test.ts`, on `initialiseRegion` in `RunResult.test.tsx`, `SettingsMenu.test.tsx` and `P3-page.test.tsx`, and on `incomingAttackRollModifiers` in `U8-boss-defence.test.ts` call through to the real code. The animation listener in `U6-overheads-heals-rifts.test.ts:280` records sequences.

Some replace a browser or engine boundary. `P3-page.test.tsx` sets the build flag with `vi.stubEnv("ARCHIVE_P3", "on")`, standing in for webpack's EnvironmentPlugin. `Cues.test.ts` stubs `navigator.userAgent` to pick the platform, and clears the SDK's cached answer in `Settings._isMobileResult`. `WhatsNew.test.tsx` stubs `Storage` reads and writes to block one key. `U1-cycle.test.ts:252` swaps the SDK's random source with `Random.setRandom` and restores it.

`U1_5-loadouts.test.ts:50` and `:130` add `MaxRollDummy` through `region.addMob`. It is a `TestNpc` that forces maximum rolls, so the loadout tests read max hits without a seed.
