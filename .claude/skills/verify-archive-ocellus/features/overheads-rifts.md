# Overheads, heals and rifts

Ocellus wears a curse overhead, drawn under his HP bar with the cache's head icons: Deflect Melee from P4 tick 8 to 32, Soul Split from 33 to 37, none at 38, Deflect Range from 39 to 60, Soul Split again from 61 to 66 and none at 67, then the same each 60-tick cycle. A player hit of the deflected style lands as 0, and the player takes 10% of the damage it would have dealt, rounded down, as its own hitsplat. While the boss hovers over the inner void, from P4 tick 8 to 29, a melee player cannot reach him and does not swing. Ranged still lands. Two ticks after a transition attack hits an unprotected player, Soul Split heals the boss by the damage it dealt, and a heal splat shows on the boss. From cycle 2, on the tick before each inner void (P4 tick 67, then every 60 ticks), two void rifts rise north of the 5x5, on (2, 2) and (10, 2). Under each rift's green HP bar a yellow timer bar of the same size fills from the left as the rift charges, and it is full on the burst tick, 23 ticks after spawn. The player can kill a rift, and every ranged or melee attack but a special attack hits it for its max hit. A rift dead by the end of tick 89 despawns, and the chat shows "Destroying the void rift empowers you!". Each rift still standing at 90 detonates, and the player takes 14 under the void flare. The damage numbers are stand-ins, and the bar's fill direction is inferred from one frame of the owner's picture.

## Sub-features

- `overhead-icon` draws sprite 440 frame 15 (Deflect Melee), 16 (Deflect Range) or 11 (Soul Split) under the boss's HP bar while the phase has that overhead. The lead-in has none, and neither has the tick before each void, S+30 and S+59.
- `deflect` logs `player-hit-judged` for each player hit on the boss with outcome `blocked`, `passed` or `invulnerable`, or `reduced` while the explosions occur ([explosions.md](./explosions.md)). A hit is judged by the overhead up on the tick it was launched, so a ranged hit launched before Deflect Range rises lands in full. A blocked hit lands as 0. Under `block-and-reflect` (default) the player takes `floor(requested / 10)` through the same damage path as every other hit, logged as `deflect-reflected` with the blocked hit's tick, style and receipt, and nothing when that is 0. `block-at-launch` blocks and reflects nothing, and `none` blocks nothing.
- `rift-max-hit` makes every ranged or melee attack but a special attack hit a rift for its max hit, with no accuracy or damage roll. The SDK still caps a hit at the rift's 44 HP. Each hit that lands on a rift logs `rift-hit` with its tick, rift, style and damage. The Twisted Bow's max on a rift is 62 in the ranged setup and 53 in the melee setup, so it kills in one shot. Ralos throws two glaives of 18 (ranged setup) or 15 (melee setup) and needs two throws. The melee setup's scythe on Jab hits a rift once a swing for 35, its max, so the second swing lands the last 9. Magic and the Division special roll as on any target.
- `hover-melee-reach` keeps a melee attack from reaching the boss while he hovers. The inner-void phase carries boss state `present-out-of-melee-reach`, and a player with a melee weapon who targets him there stands in reach without swinging until he lands at 30. The `hoverMeleeReach` choice selects `unreachable` (default) or `reachable`, which gives the hover the landed state `present-damageable`.
- `soul-split-heal` logs `boss-healed` with cause `soul-split` and the attack's ordinal 2 ticks after an unprotected transition hit, by the damage that hit applied. A protected hit heals nothing. The heal splat stays for 2 ticks.
- `rift-spawn` logs `rift-spawned` for two NPC 16678 rifts, at (-2, -4) and (6, -4) from the boss anchor (4, 6), which is (2, 2) and (10, 2) on the measured platform. They spawn at 67 and every 60 ticks after, and play spawn sequence 14896 with its sound.
- `rift-timer` draws a 30 by 5 px bar directly under the rift's HP bar, `#1e1e0c` behind and `#e0c020` from the left over the share of the 23-tick charge done: empty at the spawn tick 67, 11/23 at 78 and full at 90.
- `rift-destroyed` logs each rift whose HP was 0 at the end of tick 89, at the next boss step. The rift plays despawn sequence 14897, and the kill line goes to the chat.
- `rift-burst` logs `rift-burst` at 90 for each standing rift with the hit receipt. The rift plays detonate sequence 14898, and spotanim 4306 plays on the player.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open `http://127.0.0.1:8002/` and watch the icon under the boss's HP bar change at P4 tick 33, 38, 39, 61 and 67. Attack the boss to see blocked hits as 0. With the melee setup, click the hovering boss and watch the player wait beside him until he lands.
- Pick `Rifts` in the `Focus` selector. The drill reloads, and the rifts appear at 67. Click one to attack it. The chat sits above the focus selector.
- Pick `Transition attacks` or `Boss heals`. Let a transition attack hit without the matching prayer to see the heal.

## Gotchas

- In the inner void the risen boss's icon is above the top of the screen at the default camera. The icon shots wait for the landing at 30.
- osrs-sdk has no "I can't reach that!". The trainer's player declines a melee swing at the hovering boss the way the SDK declines any attack, by `attack()` returning false, so the player keeps the boss as its target, spends no attack delay and swings on the tick he lands. The tile it waits on is inner void, and the rifts drill runs no floor damage.
- osrs-sdk draws player overheads where the boss's HP banner covers them. The boss's icon sits under his HP bar, as the footage shows.
- A rift joins the world on the tick after the boss spawns it, because osrs-sdk adds new mobs at the next tick. The click waits for 68.
- The osrs-sdk scythe sends all three hits to a 1x1 target. The trainer's scythe hits a target once for each tile of its width, up to three, so a swing hits a rift once and the boss three times. The hits it skips never roll, and the one it keeps is the swing's first, full-damage hit, so a rift takes the scythe's full max.
- The deflect page wears the melee setup, so only Vitest proves Deflect Range blocking.
- The footage shows a white wisp travelling to the boss before the Soul Split heal. No cache spotanim matches it, so the trainer does not draw it.
- osrs-sdk has no chatbox. The kill line goes to `#game_chat`, a page element over the canvas.
- The full-fight page also logs rifts at 67. Its checks stop at 70 and do not read them.
- osrs-sdk plays a frame sound with a cache range only to a listener closer than that range, counting Manhattan tiles less one. The rift sounds have range 7. From spawn, (2, 2) is 4 tiles away and (10, 2) is 6, so the player hears both. A rift 8 or more tiles away is silent.
- 12641 has the same length as the explosion series sound 12638. The rifts drill runs no explosions, and the check reads only P4 ticks 66 to 80.
